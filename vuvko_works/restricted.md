# Restricted, permission-based, or unclear

Usable as **reference and inspiration**. Do not copy table text, artwork or data
from these into a distributed product without doing the work described here.

---

## Derelict Ship Generator (Alammo)

<https://delacannon.itch.io/derelict-ship-generator> · free · HTML5 · v1.0

Generates derelicts for Mothership using the **Dead Planet** tables. Exports the
map to PDF and to a Twine (Harlowe) game. Rated 4.9/5 over 33 ratings.

The author states plainly that it is **not affiliated with Mothership or Tuesday
Knight Games**.

**Output rights — granted, on the record.** Asked directly in the comments
whether generated maps could be used in a game published on itch.io, the author
replied that you can use the generated map images as you wish for your projects.
That is permission for the *images it produces*, from the tool's author. It is
not permission for the underlying Dead Planet table text, which isn't his to
give.

No public source repository. A commenter asked for one in order to contribute
and received no published answer; the roadmap lists a mobile version, custom
tables and AI-generated room text.

**Verdict:** study the design freely (see
[design-notes-connectivity.md](design-notes-connectivity.md)), use exported maps
per the author's grant, and treat the table *contents* as Mothership IP below.

## Mothership / Dead Planet tables — Tuesday Knight Games

<https://tuesdayknightgames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/categories/4412576523163-Mothership-Third-Party-Licensing>

**Not an open licence. Approval is required.** From TKG's own help centre, page
last updated 2026-02-26: download and fill out the license agreement attached to
the article, send the manuscript to `3pp@tuesdayknightgames.com`, and they review
and tell you whether it is approved — publish only once approved.

The agreement (2020 text, via secondary sources) requires specific copyright and
compatibility statements, adherence to quality standards, and runs for two years
renewable, with termination and dispute-resolution provisions.

Be aware there is **conflicting information in circulation**: at least one
current guide claims TKG maintains an open licence with no mandatory greenlight.
That contradicts TKG's own help centre, which I checked directly. Believe the
publisher.

Practical experience from a third-party author: TKG were friendly and prompt,
and their requested changes were entirely about how the product referred to
Mothership and about correct logo use — they asked for no changes to the text
itself.

**Verdict:** the ecosystem is genuinely welcoming — 300+ books published between
first and third party — but it runs on a signed agreement, not on CC or OGL.
Budget for the approval round trip.

## Starship Geomorphs — Robert Pearce / Pearce Design Studio

<https://travellerrpgblog.blogspot.com/2020/07/starship-geomorphs-20.html> ·
image conversion by Eric B. Smith <https://gurpsland.no-ip.org/geomorphs/>

The hand-drawn deck-plan tiles behind both rolegenerator.com's ship images and
Geomorph Shipyard's part library. Free to download.

**No formal licence is published.** What exists is a pattern of explicit
permissions:

- rolegenerator.com credits Pearce Design Studio for *expressly allowing* use of
  their content.
- On tooling built from the geomorphs, Pearce wrote that he encourages other
  developers to create similar work, **provided it be made freely available to
  everyone**.

That is a clear statement of intent, and GPL-3.0 tools like Geomorph Shipyard
satisfy it. It is not a licence grant you can rely on for a commercial or closed
product.

**Verdict:** ask Pearce directly before any commercial use. Do not bundle the
PNGs into a redistributed package — link to his blog and have users download
them, which is what Shipyard does.

## rolegenerator.com spaceship module

<https://www.rolegenerator.com/en/module/spaceship>

Proprietary, server-side PHP, no published source, no licence statement. Its
image pool is built from Starship Geomorphs 2.0 and various small craft.

Free to use through the website. Nothing about it grants redistribution rights.
Reverse-engineering its behaviour through the public endpoint is fine for
understanding; copying its asset library is not.

## Stars Without Number — Sine Nomine Publishing

<https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/230009/stars-without-number-revised-edition-free-version>

Excellent sandbox generation tooling, and there is a genuinely free PDF of the
Revised Edition intended to be shared and passed around.

**A free price is not a licence.** I could not confirm from the product pages
what reuse rights attach to the tables. SWN Revised does carry OGL-declared
content, but the scope of that declaration is set out inside the book.

**Verdict:** before lifting any SWN table, open the PDF and read its Open Game
License section and Section 15 declaration to see exactly what is designated Open
Game Content. Do not assume the whole book is open. Not verified 2026-09-09.

## Fan compendia — mixed and per-item

<https://foundryvtt.com/packages/starforged-custom-oracles> ·
<https://foundryvtt.com/packages/starsmith-expanded-oracles>

Large collections of community Starforged oracles. Licensing is **per
contributor** and genuinely varied: some CC BY 4.0, some CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, some
CC BY-NC 4.0, several "used with permission from the author", and at least one
granted for personal copying only. The module code itself is MIT.

Notable CC BY items include Eric Bright's Starsmith Expanded Oracles.

**Verdict:** rich pickings, but check every single item individually. The
aggregate module licence tells you nothing about any given table.
