This page draws deck plans out of the RPG Mobius Geomorphs PNG tiles. Nothing is bundled — point it at your own unpacked copy.

Press Load tiles… and pick the folder holding Geomorphs/, Custom-Tiles/ and Symbols/. Or serve this folder over HTTP with a geomorphs.manifest.json beside the tiles (see geomorph_manifest.py) and it loads by itself.

Click to drop on the plan · drag to move · R rotates · Del removes · Generate keeps them.
